## 🔬 Featured Research

### HFT Alpha Research Engine

Event-driven quantitative research system for studying short-horizon signals in limit-order-book data.

- Built a **C++ limit-order-book replay engine** using AAPL LOBSTER data
- Research features include **Order Book Imbalance (OBI), Order Flow Imbalance (OFI), microprice, spread, depth, and signed volume**
- Developed Python-based feature engineering and ML pipelines for alpha research
- Built historical event-replay backtesting and strategy evaluation workflows
- Investigated predictive relationships between order-flow dynamics and short-horizon price movements

**Tech:** C++, Python, LOBSTER, XGBoost, scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas

---

### Hawkes-Process Order-Flow Research

Research framework studying temporal dependencies and excitation patterns in high-frequency order-flow events.

- Modeled **self- and cross-excitation** across trades, cancellations, and order submissions
- Engineered Hawkes conditional-intensity and market-microstructure features
- Implemented chronological **walk-forward and out-of-sample validation**
- Compared Poisson, Hawkes-process, Logistic Regression, and XGBoost approaches
- Evaluated predictive information using ROC-AUC, log loss, Brier score, AIC/BIC, and likelihood-based metrics

**Tech:** Python, Hawkes Processes, LOBSTER, XGBoost, scikit-learn, Statsmodels

---

### Statistical Arbitrage Research Platform

End-to-end research platform for systematic trading strategy development.

- Applied correlation screening and **Engle-Granger cointegration testing**
- Estimated rolling hedge ratios and generated **z-score mean-reversion signals**
- Built historical backtesting with configurable entry/exit thresholds and position sizing
- Incorporated transaction-cost assumptions into strategy evaluation
- Evaluated strategies using **Sharpe, Sortino, CAGR, volatility, maximum drawdown, cumulative returns, and trade-level metrics**

**Tech:** Python, NumPy, Pandas, Statsmodels, Plotly, GCP
